Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Applies to the FreshFlow app for Android and iOS and the FreshFlow websites.

FreshFlow lets you shop at partner grocery stores for delivery, pickup or in-store "Scan & Go", share shopping lists with your family, and pay from your phone. Store teams use FreshFlow to run their stores. This page explains what we collect, why, who we share it with and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and/or mobile number, and a securely hashed password if you set one. If you sign in with Google, Apple, an email link or your company's single sign-on, we receive your name and email from that service.
  • Shopping data: your cart, orders, pickup slots, delivery address, saved offers, dietary preferences and home store.
  • Family lists: the lists you create or join, and the items you and the other members add.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by Stripe (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay), Razorpay (UPI) or PayPal. We never see or store your full card number. We keep only a label (for example "Visa •••• 4242") and the payment provider's reference.
  • Location: only while you use the store finder, to show nearby stores and their stock. It is used for that search only: we don't save it to your account or track you in the background.
  • Camera: only to scan product barcodes and receipt codes. The image stays on your phone; only the barcode number is sent.
  • Store staff only: role, store, employee code, shifts, attendance (clock-in and clock-out times), tasks, reviews and hourly wage.
  • Technical data: basic server logs (time, address of the request, error messages) to keep the service running and secure.

We do not show ads, we do not sell your data and we do not use third-party tracking or analytics tools.

Why we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To take, pay for and fulfil your orders, and to show your digital receipt at the store's exit gate.
  • To keep family lists in sync and plan the shortest route through the store.
  • To send you a sign-in code by SMS or a sign-in link by email when you ask for one.
  • For stores: to manage stock, staff, shifts and sales reports.

Who we share it with

  • The store you order from sees your order, your name and, for delivery, your address.
  • Members of a family list you join see the list, its items and your name.
  • Service providers that run parts of FreshFlow for us, only for that purpose: our hosting provider (servers and database); Stripe, Razorpay and PayPal (payments); Google, Apple and Firebase (sign-in); and our SMS provider (sign-in codes).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, sign-in codes are stored only as hashes, and the app keeps your sign-in token in the phone's secure storage.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. Order and payment records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires. When you delete your account they are kept without your name, email, phone or address.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account.
  • On the web: the delete-account page (sign in, then confirm).

This removes your name, email, phone number, address, dietary preferences, saved payment methods, notifications, carts and list memberships, and signs you out everywhere. Family lists you created pass to another member. Past orders are kept anonymously, as explained above. Store staff accounts are removed by the store owner or the FreshFlow admin team.

Your rights

You can see and correct your details on your Profile page, and ask us for a copy of your data or for deletion using the contact below.

Children

FreshFlow is not meant for children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
